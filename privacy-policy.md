# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Litbuy Spreadsheet Budget** ("we," "us," or "this site") at [litbuyspreadsheet-budget.com](https://litbuyspreadsheet-budget.com/) handles information when you visit our independent directory. This site helps visitors reach external Litbuy spreadsheet and shopping-data destinations by category, price, brand, and guide. It does not operate as a store.

## 1. Our role and scope

Litbuy Spreadsheet Budget is an independent navigation and referral tool. We do not directly sell goods, process third-party orders or payments, operate warehouses, arrange logistics, issue refunds, or provide after-sales service. Brand names, product names, images, prices, and external platform references belong to their respective rights holders. Their appearance does not mean official authorization, authenticity, quality assurance, or endorsement.

This Policy applies to this website and its pages. A linked destination has its own privacy policy and data practices.

### How this Litbuy directory works

The main interaction is a route from a budget, category, brand, or QC-reference shortcut to an external data view. A click may be recorded as a directory event so we can identify inactive routes and understand which Litbuy Spreadsheet entry points are useful. The destination, not this site, determines the item record, seller information, measurements, stock, and transaction terms.

## 2. Information we may collect

### Information you choose to provide

We do not require an account to browse this site. If you contact us, request an item, or submit information through a form that we may make available, we may receive your name, email address, message, and any other information you voluntarily include. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to answer your request.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit, our hosting and analytics systems may receive technical and usage information such as IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device type, operating system, browser type and version, language, referring URL, landing page, pages viewed, clicks, time spent, navigation path, timestamps, and error or security logs. Campaign information such as UTM parameters may be recorded so we can understand which source brought a visitor to the site.

### Cookies and similar technologies

This site and configured third-party tags may use cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons, or similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic, attribute outbound clicks, protect the site, and understand how visitors use the directory. Some technologies are session-based and others remain until they expire or are deleted from the browser. We do not use these technologies to create an account or to process checkout on this site.

## 3. How we use information

We may use information to operate and secure the directory; maintain links and page performance; match visitors with relevant category, budget, or guide routes; measure outbound navigation and referral performance; analyze traffic sources and UTM campaigns; detect automated abuse, click fraud, scraping, and attacks; troubleshoot errors; and comply with legal obligations.

If personalized recommendations or advertising measurement is enabled through a configured tag, it will be used only as allowed by that tag's settings and applicable law. The site does not promise that every tag is active on every page.

## 4. Google Tag Manager and third parties

At the time of this update, this site directly loads **Google Tag Manager container GTM-TRMBFMSD** from Google. Google Tag Manager is a tag-management service; it may load measurement or attribution tags according to the current container configuration. We have not represented that Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or other SDKs are active unless they are actually configured in that container.

Google may process technical information when its services are requested. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's information about Tag Manager](https://support.google.com/tagmanager/answer/6102821).

We may also rely on hosting, security, domain, and site-performance providers. Providers receive only the information needed to provide their services and may process it in locations where they or their subprocessors operate.

## 5. Sharing and external destinations

We may share limited technical, aggregated, or pseudonymous information with service providers that host, secure, measure, or maintain this site. If an outbound partner or referral arrangement requires click or campaign attribution, the relevant destination may receive a referral URL, campaign parameters, click time, and technical information normally sent by a browser. We do not sell a visitor's name, phone number, or email address through this directory.

When you click an external link or open an external app, the destination independently controls its collection of information, cookies, account records, payment, order, warehouse, shipping, refund, and support processes. Review that destination's privacy policy before submitting information or completing a transaction. We are not responsible for a third party's privacy practices.

## 6. Storage, retention, and security

Information may be stored on servers operated by our hosting, security, analytics, or service providers. Because providers may use international infrastructure, the exact processing location can vary. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including link measurement, security, dispute handling, legal compliance, or operational records. We generally delete or anonymize analytics and referral records when they are no longer needed, subject to provider retention settings and legal requirements.

We use reasonable safeguards such as HTTPS/TLS in transit, access controls, limited administrative access, logging, and removal or anonymization where practical. No internet service is completely secure. If we become aware of a material data incident, we will investigate, contain it, and provide notices required by applicable law.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information we hold; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; and ask us to stop using information for personalized recommendations or referral measurement.

You can also block or delete cookies through browser settings, use private browsing, clear site data, or adjust available consent and advertising controls. Blocking some technologies may affect link measurement or site features but should not prevent basic browsing.

For a privacy request, email **privacy@litbuyspreadsheet-budget.com** with the subject "Privacy Request". We may need to verify the request and will respond within the period required by applicable law. You may also contact us through a privacy or contact link displayed on this site if that channel is available.

## 8. Children's privacy

This directory is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit or intentionally collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child provided information to us, contact **privacy@litbuyspreadsheet-budget.com**. After reasonable verification, we will delete the information when required and appropriate.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the site, tracking configuration, providers, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use after an update means that you have read the revised Policy.

Questions about this Policy or privacy choices may be sent to **privacy@litbuyspreadsheet-budget.com**. Please do not send payment, order, warehouse, shipping, refund, or after-sales questions to this site; those matters must be directed to the relevant external destination.
